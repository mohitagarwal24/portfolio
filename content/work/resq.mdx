## The problem

People hesitate to donate after a disaster because they can't see where the money goes.

## What I built

A bounty board on VeChain. Relief work is posted, funded and paid out by a smart contract, and proof of each delivery is pinned to IPFS. Fee delegation means donors never need to hold gas, and Privy handles sign-in.

## Result

Won the VeChain Global Hackathon.
